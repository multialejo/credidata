# Credidata

## Audience

Credidata serves clients who use Ecuadorian identity and company data consultations, plus staff members who manage clients, credits, contributions, and payment records.

## Goals

- Help clients understand their available credits at a glance.
- Make purchasing credits and checking payment status feel safe and predictable.
- Make operational work fast for staff without hiding important states or actions.
- Work well on mobile, where clients may check balances or start a recharge.

## Platform

Web application, including mobile web.

## Requirements

- Preserve existing Spanish terminology, routes, Livewire behavior, and payment flows.
- Make balance, transaction status, validation errors, and destructive actions unambiguous.
- Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, and responsive tables.
